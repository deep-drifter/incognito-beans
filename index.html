How We Rate

Cups are for the coffee.

Cups judge what’s in the cup, the craft behind it, and how you’re treated — across more than one visit. The room gets its own marks: vibe, work-friendliness, menu and price help you search, but never earn cups.

The cup

The same test order every visit: a black coffee, a cortado, and matcha when it’s on the menu. Clean, sweet, well-textured, served at a drinkable temperature. It counts double.

Craft

What you can see from the counter: a named roaster, fresh roast dates, beans ground per order, shots pulled fresh, a clean steam wand, and a barista who knows the beans.

Hospitality

A prompt welcome, the right order, an honest recommendation when you ask, and a clean, calm space.

Consistency

No cups from a single visit. Three Cups takes repeat visits and a second inspector’s sign-off.
