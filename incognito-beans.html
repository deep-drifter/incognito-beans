Recognizing World-Class Coffee Establishments

The Purpose of Crema

CREMA was created to bring an independent, uncompromised rating standard to specialty coffee. Just as Michelin stars celebrate culinary mastery, Crema Cups evaluate establishments on drink execution, bean provenance, interior design, and genuine hospitality.

Starting across Metro Atlanta before expanding globally, Crema serves as a trusted guide for discerning coffee lovers.

Methodology & Transparency

The Four Pillars of Inspection

Our standardized evaluation criteria across every location

01. Anchor Drinks

Evaluation of core staple offerings: espresso balance, milk micro-foam quality, and batch brew consistency.

02. Atmosphere

Aesthetic identity, lighting, acoustic warmth, seating comfort, and remote-work ergonomics.

03. Hospitality

Unpretentious barista service, order accuracy, line movement, and overall community warmth.

04. Menu Essentials

Alternative milk offerings, artisanal food/pastry curation, seating capacity, and accessibility.
